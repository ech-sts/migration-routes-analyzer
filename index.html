ℹ️ About Refugee Migration Routes Analyzer

Pedagogical Purpose:
This educational web application allows students to study the arduous journeys undertaken by refugees seeking safety in Europe. By sketching migration routes and analyzing them against an invisible layer of real-world obstacles (route-obstacles), students discover the perils, border fortifications, environmental hazards, and human exploitation that refugees face.

How the Analysis Works:

  • Route Editing: Students sketch polyline graphics representing hypothetical or historical migration routes.
  • Spatial Buffering: The application buffers the sketched routes by a configurable corridor distance (e.g. 30 km).
  • Invisible Intersection: The buffer intersects with the hidden route-obstacles layer to discover which difficulties lie along that corridor.
  • Weight & Summary: Difficulties are weighted according to severity (1–10). The app aggregates the total weight score, breaks down hazards by category, and provides reflective empathy prompts.

Configurability:

All non-static parameters—such as WebMap ID, buffer distance, units, layer title, and attribute field names—can be adjusted via the Settings dialog or pre-configured through URL parameters.
